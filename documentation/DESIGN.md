# Design

## Product direction

The interface should feel modern, clean, sophisticated, and music-forward while staying out of the way of listening.

## Accessibility and color modes

- Honor system and user color modes.
- Use CSS `color-scheme` and `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep focus states visible.
- Maintain strong contrast and keyboard usability.

## Layout

- The filter system is core and should live in a collapsible bar or compact expandable panel.
- Active filters must remain visible as chips.
- Filters should never overwhelm the listening experience.

## Visual language

- Avoid visual clutter.
- Prefer subtle motion.
- Keep typography crisp and compact.
- The app should highlight the music experience and get out of the way.

## Branding

Branding is intentionally light at this stage. Focus on product clarity, trust, and usability first.
